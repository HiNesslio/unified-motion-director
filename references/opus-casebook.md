Casebook usage:
1. Pick a maximum of 2 reference mechanisms per film; choose by *scene purpose*, not trend popularity.
2. Record what is **observed** and what is only **requested in text**.
3. Create an original treatment avoiding frame-by-frame imitation and third-party assets.
4. Once source media is provided, add `DECODED_VIDEO`, timecodes and actual critique records; before that, do not assign numeric quality scores to invisible motion.

The Skillry gallery currently presents original/remake comparison controls. Comparability is a useful **method**, not proof that our tool watched either video.
