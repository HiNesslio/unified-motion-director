# Example brief — 澳泊 / ParkGo (planning demo only)

- Audience: Macau commuters / drivers; app introduction in Traditional Chinese
- Promise: Bus arrivals and parking information can be seen with one cohesive mobility interface
- Duration: 15 seconds; 9:16 and 16:9; 30 fps
- Story: One blue route dot guides the viewer from city mobility, to bus, to estimated arrivals, to parking, and resolves into the name
- Style: high-end minimal editorial, one accent blue, pale ivory canvas, substantial whitespace; authentic real UI required at render phase
- No narration. Music only, track and license not yet supplied; timing is illustrative and **not measured**
- Copy: traditional Chinese; concise labels, no full stops in big on-screen type
- Assets needed before production: approved ParkGo icon/wordmark, authentic bus/map/parking screen captures, readable interface copy, licensed BGM
- Restrictions: no stock placeholders passed as real screens, no invented ETA/count numbers, no neon, lens flare, shockwave, excessive gradients, phone mockup optional only if needed
- Deliverable for this example: concept and reviewable HTML storyboard. It **does not** contain completed animation/video or borrowed UI.
