# LOOK — ParkGo illustrative storyboard

## North star
A single moving route dot binds several mobility decisions into one calm product identity.

## Visual tokens (provisional — replace with approved app design system)
- `#F8F8F5`: paper-like light base
- `#141B25`: near-black type
- `#2675D8`: one route / state accent
- `#D8E2EA`: spare linework
- Typography: plain humanist sans, bold destination/feature noun, supporting labels small
- Depth: mostly flat 2D with modest overlapping real UI screenshots
- Composition: asymmetrical, spacious, purposeful 9:16 primary, independently framed 16:9

## Motion
- One blue dot is the **continuity carrier**
- Camera: locked until one deliberate detail zoom; never simultaneous camera pan and zoom unless visibly motivated
- Motion: gentle inertial route movement, sharp clean text mask, measured shared-element expansion
- Seam: outgoing dot → next feature focal point, no default crossfade

## Sound
- Instrumental BGM only, licensed source pending
- Key beats are illustrative until a real track is chosen

## Exclusions
No voice, no fake speed metrics, no invented parking vacancy numbers, no generic cyberpunk/HUD/glow/glitch treatment

## Verification
All UI appearances remain concept notes until real user-provided screenshot assets are available. Do not render invented UI as product truth.
